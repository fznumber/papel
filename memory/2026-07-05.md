## T8 Thread Screw — Error Found & Corrected by Claude
- **Problem**: Mirror of IZQUIERDO inverted the internal T8 thread → left screw had left-hand thread
- **Impact**: Standard T8 leadscrews are right-hand → left screw wouldn't thread
- **Fix**: `mirror()` only applied to outer profile (helices), internal thread stays right-hand in both screws
- **Verification**: 1448/1458 thread points match without mirroring (>99% overlap)
- **Status**: 7 IZQUIERDO STLs regenerated with correction, all watertight

## Variant Decision: Hex WINS over T8
Claude recommends **hexagonal rod** as definitive variant:
- Hex: 6 flat faces = large contact area for torque, very robust
- T8: thread teeth ~1mm tip, 0.9mm deep = fragile in PETG
- Both need collares/pins for axial retention (T8 doesn't save this)
- T8 risk of self-unscrewing during operation
- T8 only advantage: easier assembly by hand (no tools)
