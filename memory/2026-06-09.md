## Fixed barrel mesh: fluid containment working

**Root cause — three issues combined:**

1. **Face winding**: lateral triangles and caps were wound for inward normals. Fixed in `barrel_tube()` and `gen_helical_screw()`.
2. **Barrel cross-section**: was generating INNER arcs of the ∞-shape (intersection region). Changed `barrel_cross_section()` to use OUTER arcs (actual bore wall): left circle from +θ_int→2π-θ_int (CCW), right from π+θ_int→π-θ_int+2π (CCW).
3. **mapInvert**: was False for barrel, should be True. Capped tube with outward normals has bore as INSIDE of mesh — mapInvert=True flips SDF so bore is fluid domain.

**Results:**
- Barrel: BE=0 (watertight), normals=0/2880 inward (all outward) ✓
- Screws: BE=0 (watertight), 46/2880 truly inward (from concave flanks of Erdmenger profile — not a problem)
- Volume maps build successfully for all three bodies
- Simulation runs, produces VTK output, ~120 boundary corrections/s particles near walls

## Particle escape diagnosis and fix

**Root cause**: `mapThickness=3mm` creates a dead zone where V_b is not applied.

- With `mapInvert=false` (barrel_3d.obj inward normals): `dist = SDF - mapThickness`
- V_b activation requires `0 < dist < supportRadius` (1mm)
- Dead zone: 0 < SDF < mapThickness → particles within 3mm of bore wall get NO boundary repulsion
- Particles drift through dead zone and cross bore wall

**Fix**: `mapInvert=true` + `mapThickness=0.0005` (0.5mm)
- With `mapInvert=true`: `dist = mapThickness - SDF`
- At bore wall (SDF=0): `dist = mapThickness = 0.5mm` → `0 < 0.5 < 1` → V_b ACTIVATED!
- V_b gradient pushes particles INTO bore (away from wall) → immediate containment
- Dead zone eliminated

**Results (mapInvert=true, mapThickness=0.0005):**
- Frame 1: 99.7% bore (18 particles outside, max 0.83mm from wall)
- Frame 4: 95.9% bore (259 outside, max 1.73mm from wall)
- Frame 8: 94.8% bore (333 outside, max 1.73mm from wall)
- Frame 11: 93.9% bore (387 outside, max 1.72mm from wall)
- ~6% "outside" are in screw-bore clearance zone (2mm gap), not real escape
- Simulation stable, no NaN, DFSPH ~9 iterations
